Is TrueFriend safe to use?

Yes. TrueFriend is designed for adults 18 and older. All conversations are private and your personal data is stored securely on your device. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How is TrueFriend different from ChatGPT?

TrueFriend is built specifically for companionship. It remembers your stories, tracks your mood, plays games with you, checks in every morning and grows with you over time. ChatGPT forgets every conversation.

Can I delete my data?

Yes. You can delete individual memories, journal entries and chat history directly inside the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all your data permanently.

What languages does TrueFriend support?

TrueFriend currently supports 19 languages including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi and Russian.

Is there a free version?

Yes. TrueFriend is free to download with 50 messages per day included at no cost. Premium subscription unlocks unlimited messages and additional features.
